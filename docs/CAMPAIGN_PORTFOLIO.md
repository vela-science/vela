## Laws shared by every campaign

1. Native systems execute; Vela governs only bounded scientific-state change.
2. Verification is scoped evidence, never acceptance.
3. Only an attributed, authorized human Decision changes local Standing.
4. External source status and Frontier Standing remain separate.
5. Calls, sessions, or profiles sharing one lineage are instrumentation, not
   independent scientific episodes.
6. Every failure, discrepancy, shared dependency, and nonclaim is retained.
7. No target substitution occurs after output is observed.
8. A negative result narrows or deletes work; it does not earn a new subsystem.

## Product and science do not borrow claims

The inactive Result Dossier can be used internally to inspect completed state.
It cannot support a public usability claim until its campaign passes. Erdős
730, Astra, and Erdős 203 can produce scientific evidence without that release,
but cannot be described as Dossier adoption or Vela productivity lift.

## Reporting ceiling

Every campaign ends with one of:

- **positive:** the frozen success contract passed;
- **neutral:** useful bounded evidence, but the primary gate did not pass; or
- **negative:** the proposed route failed or was contradicted.

All three outcomes require exact inputs, reproducible artifacts, and an honest
next obligation.
