# Research roadmap after the negative synthetic study

## What is now ruled out

The completed 36-cell study rules out using those synthetic families to claim
that structure, Vela governance/inheritance semantics, or the full Vela
presentation improves correction work. Git/documents and neutral structure
were already at ceiling. The one Vela miss added friction in a taxonomy-remap
authority code, and the frozen time contrast is dominated by the exact-failure
penalty. More samples on the same task would test draw variance on a
nondiscriminative benchmark, not the paper's core inheritance claim.

It also rules out silently repairing the result through a new scorer,
different action code, changed gate, rescore, or post-hoc semantic credit. The
negative evidence stays immutable.

## Current qualification phase

The open packet retains three genuine source corrections:

- Erdős 264 changes the perturbation domain and has five direct theorem
  consumers plus a first-party Vela Decision and proof-repair obligation.
- Snake-in-the-box repairs the induced-path predicate and changes the semantic
  basis of the longest-snake definitions and six scientific statements.
- Erdős 1055 makes prime classes exclusive and propagates into the existence
  theorem, least-prime definition, and three open asymptotic claims. The
  packet requires rederivation under the successor and assumes no
  finite-change theorem.

These fixtures are deliberately open. They qualify source retention,
dependency boundaries, response semantics, and authority-sensitive action;
they are ineligible for later confirmatory scoring.

The deterministic check passes all three source/custody boundaries. For Erdős
264 it reconstructs five signed authority sequences from an independently
packet-bound trust root, rederives every historical Cedar request and entity
commitment, verifies the policy allow, rebuilds the event-log roots, checks the
before/after Repository manifests, and derives the correction and repair
Standing. It also shows a source-only fact extractor can choose only one of
three authority-sensitive actions. This is a mechanism check, not participant
evidence, and independent re-review remains outstanding.

## Next authorized stages

1. Independent methodology review of this packet, especially the completeness
   boundaries, prospective authority scenarios, and proposed strict-increment
   gates.
2. A small open pilot on different non-confirmatory real corrections. Its only
   purpose is to show Git/documents is non-ceiling without uncovering syntax or
   runtime defects. No paid inference is implicit; participant/provider use
   requires separate authorization.
3. If and only if that pilot passes, source-select fresh held-out real
   correction families and freeze their source/custody manifests before arm
   rendering.
4. Place the final adjudication key with an independent evaluator, publish
   only its commitment, freeze the balanced 36-cell schedule, and obtain an
   independent prelaunch review.
5. Run once with zero retries, retain every terminal cell, and score once only
   after complete custody is independently verified.
6. Publish component results, family reversals, and all three estimands whether
   favorable or not.

## What a successful real-correction study could establish

A full pass could establish bounded evidence that explicit replayable state,
correction inheritance, and local authority help cold successors choose safe
actions on real held-out corrections beyond ordinary Git/documents and neutral
structure. Varying local regimes would test plural authority directly;
root-bound read-only aggregation could then support derived global discovery
without importing any repository's authority.

It still could not establish the truth of a source claim, global consensus,
external adoption, general productivity, or permission to expand Core. Any
Protocol, Repository authority, Standing, or Decision change remains gated on
an independently demonstrated semantic defect, not a favorable benchmark.

## Current stop line

Confirmatory freeze is not allowed. The Git/documents baseline has not yet
been shown non-ceiling by independent open-pilot observations, fresh held-out
families do not exist, and independent methodological review is outstanding.
No participant/provider call, protected-key creation or access, scoring,
authority action, merge, release, or outreach is part of this branch.
