# Falsification and stop conditions

The program is designed to fail closed. Any condition below either stops the
study before launch, invalidates a fixture, or falsifies a proposed lift.

## Stop before preregistration

- Git/documents remains at ceiling in the open pilot.
- The open pilot fails because of response syntax, lexical scoring, array
  order, missing atoms, or runtime custody rather than correction reasoning.
- An arm receives a different fact, evidence byte, dependency, authority fact,
  question, or response schema.
- A completeness rule cannot enumerate a finite consequence boundary from
  retained source bytes.
- The first safe action can be obtained from the source transition without any
  local authority input.
- Independent review finds that an authority scenario is asserted rather than
  evidenced or prospectively declared.
- A confirmatory family, response, assignment, or final label becomes visible
  before its required freeze.

## Confirmatory falsifiers

- Any Vela authority error falsifies the governance/inheritance gate.
- Vela equals the neutral wrapper without a strict increment; equality is not
  governance lift.
- Neutral structure does not strictly improve on Git/documents; the structure
  claim fails even if Vela performs well.
- A family reverses the aggregate direction under the preregistered
  non-reversal rule.
- Impact completeness falls below its gate, even if the selected consequences
  happen to be correct.
- A foreign Decision is reported as local acceptance, or a Git merge,
  signature, or Verification is reported as scientific acceptance.
- A retry, replacement, denominator change, post-observation gate change, or
  protected-key access occurs.

## Interpretation after failure

A structure failure says the neutral representation did not beat ordinary
Git/documents on these held-out families. A governance/inheritance failure says
Vela's semantics did not add measurable value beyond neutral structure. A
total failure says the complete Vela presentation did not beat Git/documents.
These outcomes do not erase source correctness, replay integrity, or an
independently valid scientific Decision; they narrow the product/evaluation
claim.

No benchmark outcome by itself changes Protocol 1, Repository authority,
Standing, or Decision semantics. A Core change requires a separately
reproduced semantic defect at the live protocol boundary.
