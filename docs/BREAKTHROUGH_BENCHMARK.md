# Vela protocol breakthrough benchmark

Status: **north-star contract; fixture roots not yet preregistered**

This benchmark is the binary test for Vela's protocol-scale claim:

> Vela demonstrates a protocol breakthrough when a real scientific correction
> crosses independent implementations and independently governed Frontiers,
> produces the same exact bounded consequences, preserves unaffected knowledge
> and local authority, opens the correct repair work, and lets a cold successor
> continue materially faster without a central Vela service.

The active campaign may finish useful engineering before this benchmark passes.
It may not describe that engineering as the protocol breakthrough.

## Decisive scenario

The complete scenario must pass once on a preregistered mathematical fixture
and again on a held-out fixture selected after the implementation is frozen:

```text
independent producer
  -> portable Submission
independently controlled verifier
  -> scoped Verification Record
Frontier F1 authority
  -> Decision and exact root transition
clean-room implementation
  -> same replayed bounded state
later scientific correction to accepted Claim A
  -> exact affected set
  -> unaffected knowledge and independent support preserved
  -> bounded repair Obligation opened
Frontier F2
  -> foreign correction retained and checked
  -> local Standing unchanged without a local Decision
cold successor
  -> correct useful continuation materially faster
```

No participant may require Vela's hosted read model, Observatory, Canopus,
private implementation state, original agent session, maintainer credentials,
or repository-authority credentials.

## Minimum correction fixture

Freeze the complete expected answer before either implementation runs:

```text
Claim A  accepted in F1
Claim B  consequentially depends on A
Claim C  has one route through A and one independent support route
Claim D  relates to A only through a discovery-only relation
```

Apply a real scientific narrowing, supersession, retraction, or correction to
Claim A. Byte corruption and repository-only metadata changes do not qualify.

Expected behavior:

- A's predecessor and prior Decision remain retained;
- B is classified by the declared consequence of its exact dependency;
- C loses the route through A but retains the independent route;
- D is not consequentially affected;
- at least one bounded repair Obligation names its discharge condition and
  exact causal inputs; and
- F2 retains attributed foreign state without inheriting F1's authority.

The preregistration binds origin roots, relation vocabulary, traversal or
recomputation semantics, affected and unaffected sets, surviving routes,
repair Obligations, nonclaims, resource bounds, and completeness status.

## Hard gates

Every gate is pass or fail. Performance cannot compensate for a hard-gate
failure.

### Authority

- Submission, Verification, and foreign import each have accepted-state delta
  zero.
- Only an authorized local Decision changes local Standing.
- Producer, verifier, foreign Frontier, model, database, and reader authority
  never escalate.
- No human or repository-authority credential enters a producer, verifier, or
  model trust path.

### Exact causal binding

Every consequential transition resolves to full retained roots for prior
state, Claim revision, Submission, Verification, relation inputs, authority
context, Decision, Event, and result. Missing, shortened, stale, altered, or
substituted identities fail closed.

### Deterministic replay

The Rust implementation and one clean-room implementation independently derive
the same normative bounded projection from public schemas, rules, and
conformance material. The clean-room implementation may not import or wrap the
Rust implementation. Deleting every disposable view and hosted read service
must not affect replay.

### Correction without erasure

Prior Claims, Decisions, and Events remain independently verifiable. Current
Standing changes through replay of new history, never force-push, manual
database mutation, or mutable status replacement.

### Affected-set correctness

Within the frozen completeness bound:

```text
affected-set precision = 100%
affected-set recall    = 100%
```

The implementations must preserve every independent surviving route. Relation
types govern consequences; discovery, verification, authority, support,
opposition, formalization, dependency, and correction may not collapse into
one generic edge or scalar score. Unknown consequential relations fail closed.

### No silent truncation

Every incomplete computation reports its algorithm, relation classes, resource
or depth bound, omitted region, and completeness status. Cycle handling and
dependency omissions must be deterministic and visible.

### Federation

F2 may accept, reject, defer, narrow, or supersede locally. A foreign Decision
is attributed information, not local authority. One Vela-hosted service going
offline cannot make the retained transition unverifiable.

### Scientific integrity

Report these dimensions separately:

```text
artifact integrity
execution reproduction
formal or mechanical verification
statement fidelity
scientific support and opposition
review status
local Frontier Standing
foreign Frontier Standing
```

At least one Verification Record must come from a verifier independently
controlled from the producer and must bind exact inputs, implementation root,
environment, scoped property, and nonclaims.

### User value

Compare against Git plus the same files, evidence, verifier, and ordinary
documentation. Without live maintainer coaching, measure:

- time to identify the correction, affected Claims, surviving Claims, cause,
  and next repair;
- time to locate decisive evidence; and
- time for a separate cold successor to begin one correct useful action.

The median must improve by at least 20 percent on both fixtures without lower
answer correctness or more false scientific admission. Publish all
interventions, exclusions, and errors. Initial protocol-breakthrough credit
requires at least one participant external to Vela maintainers on each frozen
fixture. The current first-party campaign earns no such credit.

## Required adversarial cases

The benchmark must fail closed or return an explicitly bounded diagnostic with
no Standing effect for:

1. stale prior state;
2. wrong Claim revision;
3. missing premise;
4. unknown consequential relation;
5. discovery mislabeled as dependency;
6. altered Verification Record;
7. producer or verifier attempting a Decision;
8. foreign Decision presented as local authority;
9. incomplete traversal presented as complete;
10. nondeterministic cycle handling;
11. correction that erases history;
12. accidental loss of an independent support route;
13. mutable URL or label substituted for exact identity;
14. hidden hosted-database dependence;
15. schema or reducer mismatch;
16. tampered interchange package or mapping root;
17. shortened-digest collision; and
18. replay with an omitted canonical Event.

## Required release evidence

A valid benchmark release publishes:

- specification and frozen primary and held-out fixtures;
- origin roots, expected projections, relation rules, and Obligations;
- conformance and adversarial vectors;
- Rust and clean-room results with raw replay output;
- authority audit;
- cold-use protocol and raw timing/correctness data;
- every exclusion, failure, amendment, and limitation; and
- clean reproduction instructions.

Reports distinguish preregistered expectation, observation, implementation
defect, protocol ambiguity, scientific judgment, and benchmark limitation.

## Decision rule and maturity

The result is binary. Declare the breakthrough only if both fixtures pass every
hard gate, both implementations agree, the adversarial suite fails safely, the
20-percent cold-use threshold passes, and the external participant requirement
is met. One unauthorized Standing change, missed affected Claim,
over-invalidated route, silent truncation, replay divergence, hidden central
dependency, or failed user-value threshold is a failure.

```text
Level 0  local admission and replay kernel
Level 1  two implementations agree on correction impact and route survival
Level 2  protocol breakthrough: two fixtures plus non-escalating federation
Level 3  recurring use across independent organizations and implementations
Level 4  routine compounding across scientific systems
```

Current evidence is Level 0. The active Formal slice tests product and
statement-fidelity handling; it does not itself advance the correction
benchmark. The correction fixture, clean-room reader, second Frontier, held-out
replication, and cold-use evidence must be earned in that order.
