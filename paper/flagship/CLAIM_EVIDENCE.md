# Claim-evidence matrix

Status terms in this matrix are literal:

- **supported** means exact retained evidence and an independent byte-bound
  review support the bounded claim;
- **directional** means the observation helps choose a next test but cannot
  satisfy the registered causal gate;
- **rejected by registered gate** means exact retained evidence passed
  independent review but failed the claim's preregistered success rule;
- **open** means the program has not produced qualifying evidence; and
- **forbidden** marks wording that the available evidence cannot support.

## No-go language

The manuscript must not claim that Vela establishes global truth, gives a
controller or verifier authority, transports Standing between Repositories,
proves a theorem from a passing check, or improves scientific productivity in
general. It must not turn the failed 16-session gate into a win through
secondary metrics. It must not turn the held-out gate failures into a positive
claim or generalize one synthetic benchmark into a universal disadvantage. It
must not treat the neutral wrapper as a Vela Repository or attribute Decision,
Event, Standing, or authority semantics to that arm.
