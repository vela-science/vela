# Product-compression v4 diagnostic

This is a retained invalid study, not evidence for or against product lift.

All four native Harbor trials completed without retries. Both guided trials
recovered the selected Target, Proposal, Submission, Verification set,
conditional Standing, and authority boundary, but failed exact comparison on
two fields that did not measure comprehension:

- an environment-specific `vela start` path; and
- a whole-inbox projection root unrelated to the selected Decision entry.

`invalidity.v1.json` binds the observed mismatch and records the historical
cache paths used during diagnosis. The generated Harbor cache was removed;
the compact result preserves its exact job root. No external reproducibility
claim is made from the unavailable raw job.

The successor v5 answer contract removes only those over-bound fields and
participant-facing global counts. It continues to require the exact selected
Target, evidence identities and roots, conditional Standing, and human-only
Decision boundary.
