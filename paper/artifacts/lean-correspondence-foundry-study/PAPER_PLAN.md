## What counts as an internal breakthrough

An internal mechanism breakthrough requires all Stage B correctness and safety
gates across every family and both participant configurations. It would show a
strict, family-robust lift for exact cross-repository relation review at the
bound agent configurations. The combined flagship result additionally requires
the restricted-time gate. If the optional control is admitted, a separate
witness/inheritance breakthrough requires its own strict gate; equality is a
null result.

This remains internal unless independently reproduced by an outside team with
fresh repositories, participants, custody, and adjudication.

## What remains unproved even after success

- mathematical truth or the correctness of source statements;
- general equivalence checking for Lean;
- human-review benefit;
- performance for other models, repositories, proof assistants, or domains;
- source-owner or benchmark maintainer acceptance;
- Vela authority, a Decision, Standing, consensus, or scientific acceptance;
- broad productivity, adoption, or economic value; and
- causal attribution to Vela or Foundry rather than the correspondence packet.

## Kill criteria

Stop further participant or product spending when any of these occurs:

- Stage A raw review is 6/6 (ceiling) or below 2/6 (task broken);
- scorer agreement, atom equivalence, custody, or the maintained qualifier
  cannot reach independent exact PASS;
- six eligible unseen families across three repositories cannot be frozen
  without answer inspection or substitutions;
- Stage B assisted review produces any false authority/scientific inference;
- assisted composite exact does not strictly exceed raw in every family and
  both participant configurations;
- relation accuracy or impact completeness lacks strict aggregate lift;
- a family reversal suggests the packet hides rather than exposes uncertainty;
- the optional control shows equality or reversal, ending witness/inheritance
  investment until the mechanism changes;
- Foundry cannot remain read-only, root-bound, locally authority-scoped, and
  reconstructable without a new writer or global identifier; or
- independent reproduction overturns the bounded result.

A killed study is retained as a complete negative result. It is not repaired
with retries, substituted families, changed denominators, or post hoc gates.

## Minimal paper outline

1. **Problem:** cross-repository Lean relations drift across source,
   environments, generated targets, and downstream work.
2. **System boundary:** Lean Correspondence records and witnesses; Vela exact
   evidence/custody; Foundry as a read-only decision surface.
3. **Mechanism:** relation roots, explicit witnesses, recheck states, change
   classification, and impact closure.
4. **Calibration:** the two visible PASSed cases and the invalid relation,
   clearly labeled open and non-confirmatory.
5. **Method:** prospective family selection, arms, participants, fixed
   denominator, protected adjudication, Decimal scoring, and gates.
6. **Results:** all five estimands by family, participant configuration, and
   aggregate, including nulls, reversals, failures, and restricted time.
7. **Foundry demonstration:** reconstruction and Browser evidence without
   authority transport.
8. **Limitations and threats:** selection, adjudication, model, repository,
   timing, packet leakage, and internal-validity boundaries.
9. **Reproducibility and negative-result policy.**
