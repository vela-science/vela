# Anomaly watch and reopen gate

The foundational top-down program remains closed. These are watch signals, not
active lanes:

- a carried adaptive state beats exact-history access and a strong executable
  compilation baseline under matched resources, retention, and replication;
- a fixed generalist beats separately optimized specialists across at least
  three scientific systems under matched data, FLOPs, parameters, inference,
  and prospective/OOD validation;
- a machine constructs a new physical assay that crosses a predeclared
  identification boundary and beats expert plus optimal-design baselines;
- fixed-model, fixed-outer-algorithm work compounds prospectively beyond
  exact-history and strong artifact controls;
- a major capability effect survives scale, data, retrieval, search, tool,
  inference, external-state, tuning, human, evaluator, and leakage ablations.

A `REOPEN_CANDIDATE` requires all twelve frozen controls in the campaign
specification: large, objective, repeated, information-equivalent,
resource-matched, and not explained by packaging, retrieval, version mismatch,
hidden state, leakage, evaluator differences, or baseline incompetence.

If observed: freeze exact inputs/outputs/HEAD/branch/receipts, replicate once
unchanged, stop scaling, report to the user, and do not generate theory.
